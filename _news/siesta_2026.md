A new edition for SIESTA: International Software Engineering Summer School in Madrid

---

Last week, I had the pleasure of participating in SIESTA 2026 – the International Software Engineering Summer School.

The talks were a great source of inspiration, covering AI for Software Engineering, LLMs and agentic software engineering, deep learning, autonomous driving, and AI testing — all closely connected to my research interests.


Beyond the research, SIESTA was a fantastic opportunity to meet brilliant researchers and PhD students, exchange ideas, and enjoy the amazing city of Madrid.


You can find some snapshots from SIESTA in <a href = "https://pasinisamuele.github.io/moments/siesta26/">Moments</a>
