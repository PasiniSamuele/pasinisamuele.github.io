:dart: After Phaenomena, another Chapter for Formula USI :dart:

---

It was invited as a speaker at yesterday's AIPERITHINK by ATED - Associazione Mantello Tech ICT e Digital Ticino.
It was a great evening, full of engaging discussions.

I had the chance to present our project, "Self-Driving Cars on Interactive Dynamic Tracks", funded by the SNSF. The project connects FormulaUSI and Phaenomena, showing how interactive experiences can help bring autonomous driving and AI closer to a broader audience.

I also really enjoyed listening to the other speakers, Fabrizio Rinaldi, Natale Motta, and Stefano Garavaglia, who shared very different and complementary perspectives on how AI is shaping technology, business, and society.

A special thanks to Roberto Minelli for being the person who brought this project together, and to Luca Mauriello, Raja Baranzini, Cristina Giotto Boggia, and the entire team for all the work behind such a great event.
